## Full narration (plain text)

Welcome to Inspections Portal. Build custom inspection checklists, schedule them, capture photo evidence and auto-score Pass/Fail.

In this short tutorial, we'll walk through how to use the app.

First, register your organization. The first person to sign up becomes the administrator.

Enter your details and a password, then create the organization.

Inspections dashboard. Forms, scheduled inspections and pass/fail breakdown.

Build an inspection form. Add questions (Pass/Fail, Text, Number, Choice), set photo rules, or bulk-import from Excel.

Form library. All inspection templates; publish, assign to dates, or edit.

Schedule. A month calendar of due inspections and completed records.

Records. Searchable history of completed inspections with responses, scores and photos.

Inspections Portal — checklists that hold people to standard.. Start by registering your organization.

That's a quick tour of Inspections Portal. Thanks for watching — get started by registering your organization.
